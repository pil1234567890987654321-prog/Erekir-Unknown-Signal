## About

Erekir: Unknown Signal is a Mindustry mod set beyond the known territories
of Erekir.

The mod focuses on exploration and discovery, combining new content with
an ongoing story surrounding an unidentified signal.

Rather than simply expanding the existing technology tree, the mod introduces
new discoveries whose origins are not always clear.

## The Signal

Something has appeared beyond the known territories of Erekir.

Its origin is unknown.
Its source is unknown.
Its purpose is unknown.

Technologies, materials, and structures discovered in forgotten regions
appear to share a connection with the same unidentified signal.

The investigation continues.

## Exploration

The main progression of the mod is built around discovering new sectors
and technologies.

Each unexplored region may contain new resources, environmental conditions,
technologies, or information about the Unknown Signal.
